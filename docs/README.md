# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This repository contains the core project management guidance used by OctoAcme to plan, deliver, and improve work across cross-functional initiatives. The goal is to provide a shared source of truth for teams working on product features, services, and integrations so that projects start with clear intent, stay aligned during execution, and close with measurable learning. The material emphasizes customer value, iterative delivery, clear ownership, and transparent communication.

OctoAcme follows a lightweight lifecycle that moves from initiation to planning, execution, release, and retrospective. At the start, teams validate the business problem, align stakeholders, and define success criteria, milestones, and initial risks. Once the initiative is approved, the team translates it into a prioritized backlog, milestone plan, and Definition of Done so work can be delivered in small, testable increments. During execution, teams run daily or regular standups, track blockers, review progress, and escalate issues through clear project roles and communication paths. At release, projects verify readiness, run smoke tests, communicate outcomes, and capture lessons learned so the next cycle improves.

## Core project management processes

- Initiation: confirm the business need, identify stakeholders, define goals and success metrics, and decide whether to move forward with planning.
- Planning: create the backlog, estimate scope, define milestones, document dependencies, and align on a release plan and path to delivery.
- Execution and tracking: build in small increments, review progress in standups and weekly syncs, manage risks, and keep the project board current.
- Release and deployment: validate readiness, deploy with checks, run post-deploy verification, and communicate outcomes to stakeholders.
- Retrospective and continuous improvement: capture learnings, convert them into action items, and review improvements in follow-up project work.

## Roles and responsibilities

The project model depends on clear ownership across product, delivery, and stakeholder groups. Product managers define what should be built and how success will be measured. Project managers coordinate the plan, timeline, risks, dependencies, and communication cadence. Developers build, test, and maintain quality while participating in reviews and implementation decisions. QA/testing contributors validate acceptance criteria and quality expectations. Stakeholders provide approvals, business context, and escalation support when needed.

## Communication and quality practices

OctoAcme reinforces regular communication through weekly syncs, milestone updates, and structured escalation paths for blockers or release issues. Teams are expected to maintain a single source of truth for project status, capture risks and dependencies early, and escalate when a problem crosses team or business impact thresholds. Quality is treated as a continuous practice: CI checks, unit and integration testing, security scanning, smoke tests, and manual QA are all part of the workflow, especially before release. This helps teams reduce risk while keeping delivery focused on measurable value.
